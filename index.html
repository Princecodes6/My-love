Chapter One

How It All Started

I don't think I could ever explain exactly when everything changed. Sometimes there isn't one specific moment when you realize someone has become important to you.

It just happens slowly.

You start looking forward to talking to them. You notice yourself smiling at your phone. You start thinking about them when they're not around. And eventually you realize that this person isn't just another person anymore.

That's what happened with you, Paige.

Chapter Two

Somewhere Along The Way

Somewhere along the way, you became one of the people I care about the most.

I started realizing how much your presence could change my entire mood. A simple conversation with you could turn a bad day into something better.

And honestly, I don't think you always realize how much the little things mean to me.

The conversations. The jokes. The random moments. The times we talk about absolutely nothing and somehow those end up being some of my favorite moments.

"Out of all the places I could be, sometimes my favorite place is simply wherever I am with you."
Chapter Three

Why You

I could make a list of a thousand reasons why I love you, but somehow a list still wouldn't feel like enough.

I love the way you can make me smile without even trying. I love the way talking to you never feels like something I have to force.

I love the little things about you that you probably don't even think twice about.

And most importantly, I love that you're you.

I don't want some perfect version of you. I don't need you to change yourself into somebody else. I just want you exactly as you are.

Chapter Four

The Little Things

Sometimes love isn't some giant movie scene.

Sometimes it's staying up talking when you should probably be sleeping.

Sometimes it's laughing at something that wouldn't even be funny to anyone else.

Sometimes it's checking your phone hoping to see their name.

Sometimes it's remembering something they said days ago because you actually cared enough to remember it.

And sometimes it's simply sitting there thinking, "How did I get lucky enough to have this person in my life?"

Chapter Five

What You Mean To Me

Paige, you mean more to me than I know how to put into words.

You're not just someone I love. You're someone I genuinely care about.

I care about your happiness. I care about how you're doing. I care about the things that make you excited and the things that bother you.

I want to be someone you can laugh with, talk to, trust, and feel comfortable being yourself around.

I don't want our relationship to only be about the big romantic moments.

I want the ordinary moments too.

"If I had to choose between a thousand different memories, I'd still choose the ones that have you in them."
Chapter Six

The Future

I don't know exactly what the future is going to look like.

Nobody really does.

But I know that when I think about the future, I hope you're somewhere in it.

I hope we get to make more memories together. More laughs. More random conversations. More nights where neither of us wants to hang up.

More moments that we'll look back on someday and realize how much they meant.

Chapter Seven

If I Could Tell You One Thing

If I could make sure you understood one thing, it would be this:

You matter to me.

More than you probably realize.

I don't love you because you're perfect. I love you because you're you.

And even when life gets complicated, even when things aren't perfect, I hope you always remember that there is someone who genuinely cares about you and wants to see you happy.

The end... or maybe the beginning
